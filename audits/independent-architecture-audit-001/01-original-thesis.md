## Reconstructed original problem and hypothesis

Limited inference from the actual response: a linear succession of charge/field/law
definitions can leave the learner to infer the system. The prototype instead exposes
relationships, mechanisms, branches, loops and thought experiments, moving between
scales before mathematical formalism. The intended value is easier formation and
retrieval of a usable mental model, not accumulation of intermediate compiler objects.

Original product hypothesis, as stated retrospectively in the approved historical
context: difficult linear source material can be transformed into alternative forms
that improve understanding and recall. The artifacts support the existence of a
multi-form explanation; they do not prove the improvement claim. [evidence:H-original]

Success would mean learner benefit relative to reading the source, with important
meaning and qualifications retained. An implementation architecture is not specified
by these files. Neither current KnowledgeModel contracts nor later trust proposals
should be projected backwards onto the prototype.

## Owner-reported experience, separately

The project context says the prototype reportedly delivered strong learner value
and that later reviewed outputs were materially inferior to that experience.
These are preserved owner/project reports, not raw contemporaneous learner responses
inside the PDF/RTF. [evidence:H-original] [evidence:H-relative]

No original input source, source hash, exact prompt, model identity, request IDs,
token usage, latency, cost, participant count, comprehension score, delayed-recall
measure or blinded preference result was recovered here. The original experience
cannot be quantitatively compared with later experiments from these files alone.

## Later interpretations, not original proof

The project later used a grounded semantic intermediate representation and its
structure as an explanatory interface. Its first fixture did not establish general
extraction reliability. [evidence:D001] Early real extraction was mixed, and valid
grounding was not equivalent to correct or useful relationships. [evidence:D002]
Those are later project findings, not evidence of what the original model did internally.
The current variable-resolution objective is a later articulation. [evidence:V-objective]
