## Evidence discipline

- Historical evidence: original artifacts and what is directly visible in them.
- Current implementation: executable components, not an endorsed future design.
- Human learner evidence: owner observations, with their limited population and method.
- Research/trust findings: mechanical tests, reliability experiments and bounded proofs.
- Internal hypotheses: future design conjectures, withheld from this initial package.

Each factual phase is indexed by an evidence citation token. These tokens resolve in
[exact evidence extracts](evidence-extracts.json) to source hashes and JSON pointers
or exact decoded character ranges. They are not citations to a model's memory.
Artifact observations use PDF page locators. Analytical statements marked
“limited inference” or “open question” are not experimental results.

The [initial manifest](initial-package-manifest.json) lists every packaged file and
hash. The archive is self-contained: originals, selected unchanged learner surfaces,
source-bearing KnowledgeModels, text/ASCII outputs, core executable modules, and
exact report/owner-record excerpts. It does not contain raw project coordination,
future-plan contracts or current-team architecture choices. Source paths on excerpt
records are provenance locators, not instructions to load those excluded documents.

Preserved implementation is evidence, not a suggested architecture. Do not execute
provider code, regenerate historical outputs or retrieve linked public sources during
the initial audit. Static surfaces can be inspected locally. Some old human-review
templates and machine reports say PENDING; later owner observations are distinct
records, not edits to those originals. Screenshots, raw learner transcripts, measured
recall and population-level learning gains are not implied by a recorded verdict.

Missingness matters: the recovered prototype has no frozen input/prompt/model/run
ledger; historical selected reports are not an exhaustive all-project billing ledger;
owner review is not a blind comparative learning trial. These limitations must survive
the audit. The custodian retains full-source hashes and historical files for traceable
requests after the auditor freezes its independent diagnosis and recommendation.

The only next action authorized here is owner review of this preparation. The brief
is a proposed future handoff, not authority to perform that audit in this context.
