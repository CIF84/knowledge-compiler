# Blind learner review — future authority only

The owner is the learner, not the selector, arm builder or identity keeper. Receive
only the custodian's opaque bundle and assigned counterbalanced order. Do not inspect
repository prompts, private source-specific fidelity keys, system metrics or identity
maps during review. Recognizable source prose and native renderer differences are
possible clues: record guesses and confidence rather than claiming perfect blinding.

For each source, time only the first randomized exposure, from opening to the learner's
declared initial understanding. Record exposure position and familiarity. Do not time
later crossovers as independent first learning. Keep first-learning and later-review
preferences separate. Later-recall usefulness is an owner judgment, not demonstrated
delayed retention unless the separate frozen comprehension protocol measures it.

Use the same nine dimensions for every view: fastest understanding, clearest mental
model, information compression, nuance, later-recall usefulness, relationship visibility,
unnecessary overhead, first-learning preference and later-review preference. Record
reasons and confidence. Pairwise choices use opaque labels, with explicit TIE, NEITHER
and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; do not force a winner or collapse the dimensions into an
invented total score. Explain whether extra structure externalizes useful cognitive work
or merely adds reading/interaction overhead. No population-learning claim is authorized.

Complete `templates/learner-review.json`, including pairwise first-learning/later-review
preferences. Sign and lock the complete five-source review before any identity/system
metrics release. No learner verdict is assigned under BENCH-001.
