## Frozen arms

### S — source control
Original passage in neutral reader; headings/paragraphs preserved; direct source recovery. 0 calls/source.

### A — direct explanation
One call using only source + shared task. Complete neutral learner envelope with explanation, optional supported structures, overview/detail peer views, and source anchors. 1 call/source.

### B — plan then explain
Call 1: small plan containing organizing principle, necessary relationships/dependencies, examples/contrasts, essential qualifications, supporting spans, overview/detail mapping.
Call 2: original source + plan → same learner envelope as A.
2 calls/source.

### C0 — current compiler
Freeze current normalize/extract/dedup/model/coverage behavior, deterministic structure and representation routing, with compatible frozen renderer. Preserve actual semantic behavior and limitations; do not substitute hand-built SPEC-038 fixtures. Match common model policy only where request plumbing can change without changing arm semantics.
1 call/source maximum.

### C+ — competitive semantic compiler
Experimental benchmark arm only:
1. typed inventory extraction;
2. typed structure/relationships;
3. claim/evidence bindings;
4. explanatory composition using both admitted semantic store AND original source.

Preserve admission gates, evidence and claim access. No atomizer or semantic judge. No repair calls. Same neutral learner envelope as A/B.
4 calls/source maximum, conditional fail-closed progression.

Five-source ceiling: S 0 + A 5 + B 10 + C0 5 + C+ 20 = **40 model calls maximum**. Zero retries, repairs, model judges, enrichment calls or failed-arm substitutions.

## Shared task — exact freeze

> Using only this passage, help a new learner form and later retrieve a coherent explanation. Show the organizing idea and necessary relationships, preserve conditions and uncertainty, and keep detail and support accessible. Use prose where it works; use a diagram, table or causal sequence only where its encoded meaning is supported. Do not add outside facts or pretend a conceptual exercise is an executable simulation.

Overview/detail are peer views, not repeated destructive shortening. Labels, arrows, groupings and tables count as semantic claims.

## Future fidelity review

Prepare contract/templates only.

Before generation, two independent qualified fidelity reviewers define per source:
- essential commitments;
- material qualifiers;
- dependencies;
- at least three fixed questions including explanation/prediction and qualification.

Third reviewer adjudicates disagreements from source. Obligations remain hidden from generators.

Later fidelity scoring covers unsupported claims, omitted implications, referents, polarity, causality, quantities/units, scope/time, uncertainty, cross-resolution contradictions, misleading arrows/groupings and source-link correctness. Materially misleading mechanism or lost qualification = material fidelity failure.

## Frozen decision rule

No production promotion from five passages.

Continue a generative candidate only if fidelity-acceptable on all five and preferred over S for first learning or later review on at least 4/5, with no clear disadvantage on the other use.

Prefer a heavier candidate over A only if it has fidelity-adjusted advantage on at least 4/5 paired sources, OR avoids a material fidelity failure in A without losing useful completion.

Tie favors simpler only when fidelity/completion are comparable.

Mixed result → inconclusive + fresh versioned replication.
All generative candidates fail → retain source/reader or investigate reviewed-content model; do not relax gates.
C+ wins while C0 loses → reject wholesale anti-compiler conclusion and identify repaired capability.
A/B win while C+ only adds cost → mandatory semantic-core claim weakens.
