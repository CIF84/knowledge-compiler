# Independent source-defined fidelity keys and review

Future authority only. Two independently qualified reviewers inspect each frozen source
BEFORE generation. Neither sees architecture identity, prompts, candidate output or another
reviewer's draft. Each records essential commitments, material qualifiers, necessary
dependencies/implications and exact spans; define at least three fixed questions including
explanation/prediction and qualification, with scoring rubrics and acceptable alternatives.
Third qualified reviewer adjudicates disagreements using only the source. Freeze signed
obligations/questions/rubrics with hashes; custodian seals them from generators.

After generation, review anonymized outputs against frozen source/key. Audit unsupported
claims, omitted implications, entity referents, polarity, causal force, quantities/units,
scope/time, uncertainty, cross-resolution contradiction, misleading arrows/grouping/table
semantics, and exact source-link recovery. Anchors alone never prove meaning. Classify
materially misleading mechanism or lost material qualification as MATERIAL_FAILURE;
allow ACCEPTABLE or UNRESOLVED with exact reasons/spans. Unresolved cannot earn a
fidelity-adjusted win. Record reviewer minutes and arm guesses privately, not in learner UI.

Use `templates/fidelity-key.json` before generation and `templates/fidelity-score.json`
afterward. No obligations/questions or reviews are created from benchmark content now.
