Gene expression begins when RNA polymerase binds near a gene in DNA. During transcription, RNA polymerase uses the DNA sequence as a template to create messenger RNA. The messenger RNA then leaves the nucleus and carries the encoded information to a ribosome.

During translation, the ribosome reads messenger RNA and joins amino acids into a polypeptide. Transfer RNA brings particular amino acids to the ribosome. The order of codons in messenger RNA determines the order of amino acids in the polypeptide.

The polypeptide folds into a functional protein. Regulatory proteins can increase or decrease transcription by affecting whether RNA polymerase binds, so regulation of transcription changes how much protein the cell produces without changing the DNA sequence.
