You must be independent of the owner learner and arm builders. This readiness packet
does not authorize retrieval or selection. Do not send candidate source content to arm builders.

Freeze an ordered publisher/document candidate list and eligibility protocol first. Select
one each of mechanism, process, comparison, abstract theory and interconnected system,
across five distinct domains. Use English educational passages, stable public institutional,
revision-addressable or archival sources, whole explanatory units, 400–900 words target,
1,200 maximum including necessary context. Reject authentication/private material,
personalized high-stakes advice and meaning dependent on missing figures.

Take the first eligible contiguous passage in frozen publisher/document order. Record
every rejected candidate and exact rule. Check normalized/exact hashes, canonical URLs,
titles and substantive overlap against recovered electromagnetism, golden fixtures,
quantum/economics/software controls, 042/045 nine-source corpus and 060–068 derivatives;
exclude other revisions, paraphrases and overlapping excerpts. Escalate uncertain overlap
before admission; do not inspect arm performance. Unseen means unseen in this experimental
corpus, not unknown to model pretraining.

Freeze original bytes, URL, publisher, revision/time, passage offsets, bounded original
text, normalization mapping and hashes for all five together. Sign the source manifest
before any arm sees a passage. No replacement after failure, extra source for an easy
success, or adjustment of source scope after outputs. If a stratum cannot be filled,
preserve that missingness and seek explicit versioned authority.
