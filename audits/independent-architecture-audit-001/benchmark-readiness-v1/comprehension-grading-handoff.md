# Independent blinded comprehension grading — future authority only

Use only the pre-generation, adjudicated source-defined questions and answer rubrics.
At least three questions per source include explanation/prediction and qualification.
Keys are hidden from generators; never design questions to favor a candidate output.

The custodian delivers answers with anonymous learner identity and opaque view labels,
not architecture identity, system metrics or candidate-generation metadata. Preserve
the exposure/answer schedule so crossover familiarity is explicit. Do not supply answer
feedback that contaminates later reviews. Score each frozen rubric independently;
record unsupported/missing qualifications, disagreements and source-based adjudication.
Do not infer a winner from rubric totals or silently revise questions after outputs.

Use `templates/comprehension-grading.json`. Lock signed grades separately from learner
preferences, before identities/metrics release. Record grading minutes separately from
provider cost. No source-specific question, response, grade or effectiveness claim is
created during BENCH-001 readiness.
