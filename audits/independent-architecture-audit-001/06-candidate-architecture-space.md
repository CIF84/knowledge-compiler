# 06 — Independent architecture working space

Intentionally no current-team candidates, menu, outcome labels, ranking or target pipeline.
The owner amendment supersedes the predefined scaffold in the preparation contract.

After reviewing original purpose and frozen evidence, the fresh auditor should derive
competing architectures independently. The current implementation inventory is evidence
of what exists, not a required design, a candidate shortlist or a constraint on invention.

For each independently derived alternative, the auditor should record its own:

- causal account of how it delivers learner value;
- trust boundary and what remains uncertain;
- components and dependencies justified by evidence;
- complexity, calls/tokens/latency and unknown economic quantities;
- engineering effort, scaling assumptions and failure modes;
- discriminating evidence and conditions that would falsify it.

Do not populate this working space in the implementation context. Freeze independent
diagnosis, alternatives and recommendation before any current-team hypotheses are
revealed for comparison. No architectural outcome is assigned here.
