# 02 — Evidence timeline through SPEC-068

This is a findings timeline, not a count of implementation accomplishments. “Supports”
below means support within the frozen sample. “Limits” does not mean every alternative
algorithm or architecture has been falsified. Owner reports and machine evidence remain
separate; no phase establishes population-level learning efficacy.

## Early KnowledgeModel, relationships, structure and representations (001–005)

- Mechanical: a deterministic fixture established model/reference/provenance contracts;
  real five-domain extraction was MIXED; explicit predicates improved reviewed type/direction
  while endpoint/polarity/state errors persisted. Deterministic structure composition inherited
  these limits. [evidence:D001] [evidence:D002] [evidence:D003] [evidence:D004]
- Human: SPEC-005 owner reported improved orientation, engagement and desire to use the
  representations. This is single-owner qualitative evidence. [evidence:H005]
- Supported hypothesis: grounded graph structure can support useful inspected views.
- Limited hypothesis: schema-valid/exactly quoted graphs are not thereby semantically correct.
- Unresolved: unseen-source reliability, general learner efficacy and the minimum necessary
  architectural machinery; none follows from this initial acceptance.

Later endpoint/proposition and assertion experiments changed the representation substrate;
those changes are visible in the frozen executable inventory. They do not retroactively
validate the original prototype or all future transformations.

## SPEC-053–059 representation and cognitive utility

- Mechanical: all 98 claim-only items were NOT_CONSIDERED; enabling claim focus yielded
  98 truthful-prose fallbacks. Experimental typing then produced 53 richer forms and 45 prose
  plans. The utility gate classified 87 low, seven strong and four unsafe/unsupported claim
  opportunities, alongside four structural controls. These are rule decisions, not cognition
  measurements. [evidence:M053.claim_path_counts] [evidence:M054.outcome_distribution]
  [evidence:M055.richer_than_prose] [evidence:M057.claim_utility_outcome_distribution]
  [evidence:M057.structural_control_outcome_distribution]
- Human: a twelve-case 056 review was audited post hoc; promising comparisons were retained
  while low-value richer forms were suppressed. Sixteen-case 058 owner verdict confirmed the
  utility distinction but left form unresolved; eighteen-case 059 supported enriched prose,
  with selection refinement still required. [evidence:M057.spec056_owner_review_audit]
  [evidence:H058] [evidence:H059]
- Supported: access to claims and semantic safety do not by themselves justify richer form;
  some owner-reviewed comparison/enriched-prose presentations were promising.
- Limited: renderer availability and richer-than-prose counts are not benefits; post-hoc alignment
  is not an independent efficacy test.
- Unresolved: generic selection criteria and meaningful cognitive gain across unseen structures.

## SPEC-062 grouping/schema

- Mechanical: six-source schema experiment recorded 244 semantic items, 46 blocks and
  95 implications preserved under its audit. [evidence:M062.preservation_summary]
- Human: raw schema was not cognitively useful; S1/S2 distinction was not perceptible;
  compiler machinery remained exposed. [evidence:H062]

- Limited: schema presence is not a usable learner abstraction; the artifact-load repair was
  a surface repair, not evidence of a different schema experiment.
- Unresolved: whether the problem is projection or insufficient underlying conceptual abstraction.
