# 08 — Fresh independent auditor brief

Use only the verified initial archive in a new context without project-design history.
Do not load the surrounding repository's coordination, contracts or future hypotheses.
This brief is prepared for later owner-approved use; no audit is executed by its preparer.

You are an independent architecture auditor who did not design Knowledge Compiler.
Reconstruct the original product thesis from the original PDF/RTF and traceable evidence.
Evaluate convergence toward intended learner value, keeping historical artifacts, current
implementation, human experience and research/trust findings epistemically separate.
Do not assume code, abstractions, current architecture or sunk investment deserve preservation.
Equally, do not mistake missing outcome measurements for proof that a component has no value.

Inspect the original PDF directly. Its visible output is primary historical evidence;
its rhetorical effectiveness claims are not learner data. Owner reports are limited
qualitative evidence. Do not retroactively assign current architecture to the prototype.

Derive competing architectures independently from first principles after reviewing the
objective/evidence. You are deliberately not supplied current-team candidates or an
outcome menu. Account for possible confounding, contradictory evidence and missingness.
Proposed contracts in the inventory are evidence of plans, not validated architecture.

Answer:

1. Has demonstrated learner value improved relative to the original experience? What
   can and cannot be compared given the missing original inputs/measurements?
2. What was actually proved, narrowly supported, limited or left unresolved through 068?
3. Which implemented components have direct human product-value evidence, and how strong is it?
4. Which have only trust/reliability or engineering/research evidence?
5. What minimum architecture is justified by evidence today? Derive alternatives yourself.
6. What are each alternative's complexity, cost/latency assumptions and missing measurements?
7. Which assumptions might be locally rational but globally unsupported?
8. What merits retention, removal or re-testing, and what evidence would reverse those judgments?
9. What unexecuted blind benchmark would discriminate your leading alternatives? Independently
   freeze arms while retaining the neutral source/rubric/identity controls in document 07.
10. What is your recommendation in your own terms, with explicit confidence, strongest
    counterargument and the evidence that would falsify it?

Trace material claims to evidence IDs/page locators; label inference and uncertainty.
Do not equate tests, artifact counts or trust containment with learner progress.
Do not treat zero judge calls as a semantic-judge result. Neither perform provider/retrieval
calls nor generate new benchmark outputs under this preparation's authority.

Freeze and identify your diagnosis, independently derived alternatives, benchmark design
and recommendation before asking the custodian to reveal current-team hypotheses. Only
then compare your independent account with that historical material in a distinct addendum.
Do not silently revise the independent record after disclosure.
